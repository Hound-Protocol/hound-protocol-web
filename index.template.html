How we work

A passion project, not a boot camp. No deadlines, no time pressure. What we ask for is care.

01

Solid hierarchy

Everyone knows who decides what. Authority only flows down, and only in its own kind: social, technical or organizational.

02

Open communication

Anyone can talk to anyone, at any level. Questions, ideas and disagreements happen in the open by default.

03

Transparency

Decisions are written down with who made them and why. Roles are public. Work is credited to the person who did it, and to the tools they used.

// 0303

Structure

Organized like a unit: four departments, keycard levels from LV2 to LV7, and contractors graded E to S++ in each field.

LV6

Administration

Direction, accounts, community and moderation, recruiting.

LV5

Development

Architecture, code, merges and contract scope.

LV4

Quality

Testing on Windows and Linux, reviews, release sign-off.

LV3

Contractors

Take a contract, deliver it, leave whenever you like.

Careless work and skipped duties earn infractions at every level, leads included. Three suspend, appeals are always open. Full rules: the handbook.
