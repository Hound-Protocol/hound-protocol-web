# How we work
Status: Current · Updated: 2026-10-26 · Applies to: everyone

Hound Protocol is a non-commercial, community-focused organization. It is not a company or a studio. It builds ambitious projects, one repository and one team per project, under shared rules.

## The model: a contract network
People come and go. The work must not depend on any one person. So work is split into **contracts**: small tasks with clear acceptance criteria. Anyone can take one, deliver it and leave when they like ([HP-SOP-001](handbook/HP-SOP-001-contract-lifecycle.md)).

## Four departments
| Department | Job |
|---|---|
| **Administration** | Direction, decisions, accounts, legal stance, funding, community, moderation |
| **Development** | Technical direction and the core of each project |
| **Quality** | Checks every delivery and every release |
| **Contractor** | Outside contributors who take contracts |

Details: [Departments and Grades](departments.md).

## Three principles
- **A solid hierarchy.** Everyone knows who decides what ([HP-POL-007](handbook/HP-POL-007-chain-of-command.md)).
- **Open communication.** Anyone can talk to anyone, in public by default.
- **Transparency.** Decisions say who made them and why. Moderation is logged.

## Care, not deadlines
There are no deadlines. Nobody is penalized for being slow, taking a break or leaving a contract. We ask for care: check your work before it reaches others ([HP-POL-009](handbook/HP-POL-009-accountability.md)).

## AI help is welcome, credited and limited
AI tools may help with code, text and tools. AI agents are credited as tools, never as authors with authority. Generative art, music, sound and voices are not accepted anywhere ([HP-POL-002](handbook/HP-POL-002-ai-use-policy.md)).

## Every project
- Has its own repository, documentation and team.
- Follows the rules in this Documentation.
- Adds its own rules only where its situation needs them, in its own documents ([HP-GDE-021](handbook/HP-GDE-021-project-documentation.md)).
- Starts through a short checklist ([HP-SOP-013](handbook/HP-SOP-013-starting-a-project.md)).
