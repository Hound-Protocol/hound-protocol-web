# How to Contribute
Code: HP-GDE-019 · Version 2.0 · Effective 2026-10-26 · Owner: Development · For: everyone who wants to help

These are the contribution guidelines: how contributing works from the first look to the signed record, and the rules everyone follows. They are the same in every project.

**Not sure you can contribute?** Start with [Can I Contribute?](HP-GDE-017-can-i-contribute.md). Almost everyone can.

## 1. What you can do now
It depends on the project: see [Projects](../projects.md). Testers, reviewers, and help with documents and research are welcome early. Contracts open when a project says so. Ask on the [Discord](HP-GDE-008-discord-members.md).

## 2. Get ready (once)
1. A GitHub account with two-factor turned on ([GitHub Basics](HP-GDE-014-github-basics.md), [Account Security](HP-GDE-010-account-security.md)).
2. Read the [Contributor Policy](HP-POL-001-contributor-policy.md) and the [Third-Party Rights Policy](HP-POL-003-third-party-rights.md).
3. Set up your tools with the [Contributor Quick Start](HP-GDE-003-contributor-quick-start.md) and the project's own setup notes.
4. Sign in on the website: your Profile shows your keycard and what your clearance opens.

## 3. Taking a contract
1. **Pick** an open contract on the project's board. Difficulty (Easy, Medium, Hard) tells you what to expect; it is not a gate. An Easy one is a good start.
2. **Claim** it: sign in, then **Claim** on the card, or comment `/claim` on the issue. You appear in the card's **Contributors** list at once. **Unclaim** leaves it. Several people can claim the same contract; say hello and split the work.
3. **Work** on a branch in your fork. Sign off every commit (`git commit -s`).
4. **Open a pull request** that says `Closes #number`, and fill in the template, including whether you used AI tools ([Working with AI Assistants](HP-GDE-018-working-with-ai-assistants.md)).
5. **Review:** Quality checks it against the acceptance criteria and merges it. Changes asked in review are normal.
6. **Record:** you get a signed record, and the contract counts toward your grade in its field ([Grades, Records and Hound IDs](HP-GDE-009-grades-records-hound-ids.md)).

The full lifecycle is in [HP-SOP-001](HP-SOP-001-contract-lifecycle.md), and review in [HP-SOP-002](HP-SOP-002-pull-request-review.md).

## 4. Everything goes on the board
All work appears on the project's board, whoever does it, the owner and AI-assisted work included. Each card shows who is working on it and, once finished, who completed it ([HP-SOP-003](HP-SOP-003-release.md)).

## 5. The rules
| Rule | Why |
|---|---|
| Pull requests only. Nobody pushes to `main` | Every change is reviewed and checked |
| No protected files, no copied code | Our projects stay legal and open ([HP-POL-003](HP-POL-003-third-party-rights.md)) |
| AI tools are welcome for code and documents; no generative AI art, music or voices | Credit the tool; art comes from people ([HP-POL-002](HP-POL-002-ai-use-policy.md)) |
| Nothing attacks a company's services or cheats in other people's games | We build our own things ([Code of Conduct](../code-of-conduct.md)) |
| Leaving is fine; post a short handoff note first | Someone else can pick it up |
| Be kind; disagree with the work, never the person | [Code of Conduct](../code-of-conduct.md) |
| A project may add rules | They are in the project's own documents |

## 6. What you get
- **Credit:** your name on the contributor wall and in signed records, with any AI tools you used named alongside.
- **Grades:** E to S++ in each field, from completed contracts. Grades never go down and give no authority over others.
- **Clearance:** a completed contract earns the **LV3** contractor card; higher cards come with a department role.

## 7. When something goes wrong
- Stuck or unsure: ask on the issue or on Discord. Nobody is penalized for being slow or for asking.
- Careless work and skipped duties can earn infractions, at every level; appeals are always open ([HP-POL-009](HP-POL-009-accountability.md)).
