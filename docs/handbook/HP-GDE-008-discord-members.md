# Discord Member Guide
Code: HP-GDE-008 · Version 2.0 · Effective 2026-10-26 · Owner: Administration · For: everyone on the Hound Protocol Discord

The Discord server is where the team talks, tests and plans. It is organized like a unit: departments with their own channels, keycard roles and contractor grades, plus a space for each project.

<!-- hp:join-discord -->

## Joining, step by step
1. **Get Discord.** Use the app from discord.com, or discord.com/app in a browser.
2. **Make an account** if you do not have one, and confirm your email.
3. **Turn on two-factor authentication:** User Settings → **My Account** → **Enable Two-Factor Auth**. Moderation channels require it, and it protects you ([HP-GDE-010](HP-GDE-010-account-security.md)).
4. **Open the invite:** **https://discord.gg/QTpJ89kMCM**. Choose **Accept Invite**.
5. **Onboarding:** Discord shows a short setup. Pick what you are here for; you can skip it and pick later under **Channels & Roles**.
6. **Read #rules and #welcome.** By staying, you agree to the [Code of Conduct](../code-of-conduct.md).
7. You now hold **LV2 · Member**, which opens the community channels. Say hello in **#general**.

**Tips**
- **Notifications:** right-click the server icon → **Notification Settings** → **Only @mentions**, then follow **#announcements** if you want release news.
- **Nickname:** right-click the server icon → **Edit Server Profile** to use the same name as on GitHub.
- **Safety:** nobody from Hound Protocol will ever ask for your password, a login code, a token or payment. Report anyone who does to a moderator ([HP-POL-004](HP-POL-004-security-policy.md)).

## Channels
| Category | What it is for | Who |
|---|---|---|
| INFO | Welcome, rules, announcements, roadmap, FAQ | Everyone |
| COMMUNITY | General talk, showcase, suggestions, off-topic | Everyone |
| QUALITY | Test sessions, bug reports, review queue, release QA | Testers and the Quality Department |
| CONTRACTORS | Contract board, lounge, handoffs | Contractors |
| DEVELOPMENT | Dev log, architecture and technical channels | Development, readable by contractors |
| ADMINISTRATION | Admin board, legal and funding, moderation log | Administration and moderators |
| Project categories | One per project, for channels that only matter to that project | Everyone, or the project team |
| VOICE | Voice rooms | Everyone |

Channel names change as projects change. The current list is in the server.

## Roles
- **Keycards** (LV2 to LV7) show your access level.
- **Rank E to S++** roles show a contractor's overall grade, their best grade in any field ([HP-GDE-009](HP-GDE-009-grades-records-hound-ids.md)). They are cosmetic and give no authority.
- **Unit roles** show what someone works on.

How to get them:
- **Tester:** say hello in the test channel and tell us your system. Administration gives you access.
- **Contractor:** complete a contract. A records signer gives you **LV3 · Contractor** and your grade role.
- **Department member:** by invitation from Administration ([HP-SOP-007](HP-SOP-007-onboarding-offboarding.md)). Staff seats are filled once a project has a playable prototype.

## Who decides what
Moderators and Administration decide on conduct. Development decides technical matters. Anyone can talk to anyone, and you can appeal a decision one level up ([HP-POL-007](HP-POL-007-chain-of-command.md)).

## Rules in short
- Be respectful. No harassment, hate or spam.
- No piracy, cracks or files you may not share.
- Nothing that attacks a company's online services or cheats in other people's games.
- Never share passwords, tokens or keys, not even in DMs to team members.
- Some projects ask for quiet until they are ready. Ask Administration before promoting a project.
