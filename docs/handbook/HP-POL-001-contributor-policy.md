# Contributor Policy
Code: HP-POL-001 · Version 2.0 · Effective 2026-10-26 · Owner: Administration · Applies to: everyone who contributes, to any project

## 1. Purpose
This policy sets the rules every contributor accepts, whatever their department, grade or project. It protects contributors, players and the projects.

## 2. Who can contribute
- Anyone who accepts this policy, the [Code of Conduct](../code-of-conduct.md) and the [AI Use Policy](HP-POL-002-ai-use-policy.md).
- A project may ask for more where its work needs it, for example owning a legal copy of a game you test. It says so in its own documents ([HP-GDE-021](HP-GDE-021-project-documentation.md)).
- Staff seats are filled once a project has a playable prototype. Testers and reviewers (Quality) are welcome before that. Contracts open when the project says so ([ADR 0009](../decisions/0009-departments-and-ranks.md)).

## 3. Your contributions
1. **Sign off every commit** with `git commit -s`. Your sign-off certifies that you wrote the work or have the right to submit it, and that you license it under the project license.
2. You keep the copyright in your work. The project may use, change and redistribute it under its license ([HP-POL-008](HP-POL-008-licensing-and-attribution.md)).
3. You are credited in the commit history, on the contributor wall and in your signed records.
4. Work that does not meet its acceptance criteria may be sent back or closed. That is not a judgment of you.

## 4. What you must never do
- Commit files that belong to someone else and that you may not share: game files, extracted assets, or anything derived from them ([HP-POL-003](HP-POL-003-third-party-rights.md)).
- Copy code without a license that allows it.
- Touch a company's online services, or other players' online sessions, in ways their terms forbid.
- Share tokens, passwords, keys or other people's personal data, in any channel.
- Present someone else's work, or AI output you have not reviewed, as checked work.

## 5. Conduct
- Be respectful and specific. Criticize work, not people.
- Keep discussion in the right channel. Decisions go into pull requests and decision records, not private messages.
- Disagreements go one level up within the same kind of authority, and finally to the project lead, who decides ([HP-POL-007](HP-POL-007-chain-of-command.md)).

## 6. Leaving
You can leave at any time. If you hold a contract, post a short handoff note on the issue first ([HP-SOP-001](HP-SOP-001-contract-lifecycle.md)). Access is removed the same day you leave a seat or department ([HP-SOP-007](HP-SOP-007-onboarding-offboarding.md)).

## 7. Breaches
Careless mistakes, such as merging broken work without checking it, are handled by the [Accountability Policy](HP-POL-009-accountability.md): notes, infractions, and a suspension at three, with appeals and no time limits. Deliberate breaches are handled under the [Community and Moderation Policy](HP-POL-005-community-moderation.md). Committed protected files, leaked secrets or copied code are removed at once and may lead to removal from the organization.
