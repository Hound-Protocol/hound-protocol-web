# Brand and Media Guide
Code: HP-GDE-011 · Version 2.0 · Effective 2026-10-26 · Owner: Administration · For: everyone who makes graphics, posts or videos for Hound Protocol

How to use the Hound Protocol name, logo and colors. The full reference is the [Brand](../brand.md) page. A project's own brand rules are in that project's documentation.

## The brand
| Brand | Colors | Mark |
|---|---|---|
| **Hound Protocol** (the organization) | White, black and red | The hound in a black hexagon, with a red slash, eye and collar |

Files are in `assets/brand/` of the website repository: full color, one color (`-mono`), light backgrounds (`-light`), lockups with the name, and a 512 px avatar.

## Do
- Use the files as they are. Keep clear space around a mark equal to a quarter of its width.
- On photos or busy backgrounds use the one-color files.
- Keep organization and project brands in their own colors.
- Credit sources for anything you did not make yourself.

## Do not
- Stretch, rotate, recolor, add effects to or redraw the marks.
- Use another company's logos, fonts, artwork or screenshots of their logos, or anything that could look official ([HP-POL-003](HP-POL-003-third-party-rights.md)).
- Use **generative AI** images, art, music or voices ([HP-POL-002](HP-POL-002-ai-use-policy.md)). AI may operate tools, such as layout, but the artwork must be made by people or drawn as vectors from our own designs.
- Put third-party stock graphics into a logo. Stock licenses do not allow logo use without a paid license, and some forbid sharing their source files in a public repository.

## Third-party graphics
If you use a free or bought graphic somewhere else, such as a website background:
1. Check its license: is it allowed for websites, is credit required, and is it marked as AI-generated?
2. Commit only the finished image, never the source file, if the license forbids redistribution.
3. Add the credit to the project's `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` and wherever the license asks.

## Posting about a project
Some projects ask for quiet until they are ready. Ask Administration first. When you post, say what the project is and, if it builds on someone else's product, that it is an unofficial fan project and not affiliated with them.
