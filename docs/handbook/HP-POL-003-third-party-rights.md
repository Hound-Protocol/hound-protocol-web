# Third-Party Rights and Clean-Room Policy
Code: HP-POL-003 · Version 2.0 · Effective 2026-10-26 · Owner: Administration · Applies to: all research, code and content, in every project

## 1. Purpose
Staying independent and respectful of other people's rights keeps every project alive. This policy sets the working rules. It is not legal advice. A project that works close to someone else's product adds its own rules in its own documents ([HP-GDE-021](HP-GDE-021-project-documentation.md)).

## 2. Files and content that are not ours
- **Nothing that belongs to someone else and that we may not share goes in a repository, issue, Discord channel or release.** That includes game or program files, extracted scripts, textures, models, audio and text.
- Research describes behavior **in our own words**, with the version it was observed on. It never pastes protected content.
- If a project must study a product for interoperability, it does so on the researcher's own legal copy, within the law, and records the legal basis in its own documents. Results are used only for that project.

## 3. Other people's code and projects
| Source | Rule |
|---|---|
| Open source with a compatible license | May be reused with attribution, in a separate folder, with its license kept |
| Source with no license | Read for understanding only. Copy nothing |
| License with restrictions we cannot accept | Read for understanding only. Copy nothing |
| Closed tools | Use on your machine. Never redistribute, never copy |

When you learn something from another project, credit it in the research note.

## 4. Names, logos and trademarks
- Never use another company's or project's logos, artwork, music, characters or trademarks in our branding.
- A product's name may be used in plain text to say what a project works with, together with an "unofficial, not affiliated" statement.
- No commercial use of someone else's property. Projects that build on it stay non-commercial.

## 5. Our own work
- Each project states its license ([HP-POL-008](HP-POL-008-licensing-and-attribution.md)). Until it does, the repository is visible but all rights are reserved.
- The design and text of our websites belong to Hound Protocol and are not covered by a code license unless stated.

## 6. Reporting
If you spot copied code, protected files or a rights problem, tell Administration privately. It is removed first and discussed after.
