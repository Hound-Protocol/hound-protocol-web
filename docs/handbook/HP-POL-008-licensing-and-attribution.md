# Licensing and Attribution Policy
Code: HP-POL-008 · Version 2.0 · Effective 2026-10-26 · Owner: Administration · Applies to: all code, documents, graphics and contributions, in every project

## 1. Purpose
Anyone may build on our work, nobody may claim it as their own, and every piece of work shows who made it and with what.

## 2. Our licenses
| Material | License |
|---|---|
| Documentation and text | CC BY-SA 4.0 |
| Names, logos, emblems, avatars | Reserved. Use them unchanged only to refer to the project or organization they belong to |
| Code | Set per project. Each project states its code license, and any additional permission it needs, in its own documents. Until it does, all rights are reserved |
| Closed, proprietary projects | Allowed when the project's own decision says so. The repository stays private |

The organization's own websites use GPL-3.0-only for code and CC BY-SA 4.0 for text.

## 3. Your contributions
- By signing off a commit (`git commit -s`) you confirm you have the right to submit the work and license it under the project's license.
- Keep the copyright and license notices in files you change.
- Do not add code or text under a license that conflicts with the project's.

## 4. Credit for people
- Every completed contract is credited to the person who delivered it, in a signed record ([HP-GDE-009](HP-GDE-009-grades-records-hound-ids.md)).
- Research notes and documents name their author.
- Removing or changing someone's credit is not allowed, except to correct a mistake with their agreement.

## 5. Credit for AI agents
- Work done with an AI agent is credited to the person, **with the agent named** as their tool, for example "Forge77, with Claude" ([ADR 0015](../decisions/0015-ai-agents-and-attribution.md)).
- Keep the agent's commit trailers. Tools detect them and show them on pull requests and records.
- Hiding AI use on purpose breaks this policy and the [AI Use Policy](HP-POL-002-ai-use-policy.md).

## 6. Third-party material
- Before using anything you did not make (code, fonts, graphics, sounds), check its license allows our use.
- Never use generative AI art, music or voices ([HP-POL-002](HP-POL-002-ai-use-policy.md)).
- Stock graphics may only be used where their license allows: usually websites, with credit, never in a logo, and without committing the source file when the license forbids redistribution ([HP-GDE-011](HP-GDE-011-brand-and-media.md)).
- Record every third-party item and its license in the project's `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`.

## 7. Forks
Where a project is open source, forks are welcome. They keep the licenses and credits, publish their source, and use their own name and logo so people can tell them apart.

## 8. Breaches
Missing credit or a license problem is fixed first and discussed second. Deliberate removal of credit or copying of protected material is handled under the [Contributor Policy](HP-POL-001-contributor-policy.md).
