# Code of conduct
Status: Current · Updated: 2026-10-26 · Applies to: everyone, in every project and every space

Hound Protocol is an open community. Everyone who takes part, on GitHub, Discord or anywhere else the organization is present, agrees to these rules.

## Expected
- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give and accept critique of work, not of people.
- Credit others' work and sources.
- Help newcomers find their way.

## Not accepted
- Harassment, insults, threats, or discrimination of any kind.
- Sexual content or unwelcome advances.
- Sharing someone's private information.
- Piracy, cheating in other people's online games, or attacks on any company's online services.
- Repeated disruption after a moderator has asked you to stop.

## Enforcement
1. A moderator may remove content and give a warning.
2. Repeated or serious cases lead to a temporary or permanent ban from all spaces ([HP-POL-005](handbook/HP-POL-005-community-moderation.md)).
3. Reports go to the moderators privately (the Discord moderator role, or a private message to a seat holder; until moderators are appointed, the project lead). Reports are kept confidential.

Moderators who are involved in a report step back from handling it.
